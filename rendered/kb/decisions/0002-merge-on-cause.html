0002 · Duplicates share a cause

owner lbojalkowski-bf proposed reviewed 2026-10-28 · 1 days ago

Date: 28 October 2026 · Deciders: support desk · Replaces: 0001

Context

Decision 0001 merges reports on subject alone. #4471 shows the cost: reports about three different problems use the same words (“Date is wrong”), so they landed in one ticket marked known, with one fix queued for one of them. See what #4471 hid. Engineering merges separate tickets later; nobody splits merged ones.

Decision

Two reports are the same issue when they share a cause: the tenant (its locale and booking form) and the channel, as causes.ts reads them. The subject line and the intake tag do not decide it. A report the desk reads differently from its cause (desk-notes.json) gets its own ticket.

Consequences

  • Merging needs the tenant directory, not only the subject. The rule already runs in causes.ts.
  • Big tickets stay one problem each, so a queued fix closes the whole ticket.
  • 0001 is not edited; this record replaces it.
